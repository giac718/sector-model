How to read the columns

Rel 3m is the 3-month return minus the sector ETF's return (percentage points); vs 20 EMA is the close's distance from its 20-day EMA. Earnings is the next report date and how many days away it is, highlighted within 14 days. Stop is the 50-day EMA minus 0.5 ATR for longs and the 20-day EMA plus 0.5 ATR for puts (14-day ATR). Risk % is the distance from the close to the stop, and ATRs is that same distance in ATRs.
